Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setup, sending, and ongoing management.

Do I need to purchase any software or email tools?

No. Your flat monthly service fee includes the sending software, delivery infrastructure, and complete campaign management. You do not need to buy Mailchimp, Klaviyo, SendPulse, or any other third-party platform.

What do I have to do each month?

Virtually nothing. We write the copy, build responsive HTML emails, configure your localized booking links, test deliverability, and execute the sends. You simply watch repeat bookings and holiday sales roll in.

How does sending from our True REST domain work?

Emails send directly from your official `@truerest.com` domain address. Corporate simply verifies three standard DKIM CNAME records. Corporate retains 100% control of the domain and DNS at all times, with zero disruption to regular corporate email.

How do you handle client lists and privacy?

Your customer database belongs 100% to your clinic. Lists are isolated in dedicated, encrypted databases, scrubbed for bounces, and never shared or co-mingled with any other business.

What happens after the initial 12-month agreement?

After completing your first 12 months, your account automatically transitions to a month-to-month agreement. You retain your locked-in preferred rate ($150/mo) and are not increased to the standard month-to-month rate.

How soon can my location launch?

Once corporate verifies the DNS records and you provide your client list export, your location can be fully active and scheduled within 48 to 72 hours, fully prepared for Black Friday.

Note for Corporate Technology & Leadership (Pat, Stacy, & Amir):

This program requires zero software licenses or administrative overhead from corporate. Setup takes less than 5 minutes: Stacy simply adds 3 standard DKIM CNAME records to `truerest.com`. No MX records are changed, zero email routes are modified, and corporate maintains full domain governance.
